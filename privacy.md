# Privacy Policy for Megatron Video

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Megatron Video is a private, single-user desktop application operated by Ivan Sikuten. It is used only to manage and publish videos on YouTube channels owned by the operator. It is not offered to the public.

## Data the app accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing schedules, and read view and performance statistics for the operator's own channels.

## How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are used only to publish the operator's videos and to plan future content. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data about YouTube viewers or other users.

## Google API Services

Megatron Video's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service at https://www.youtube.com/t/terms and the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy applies.

## Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or through the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, locally stored tokens and data are deleted within 30 days.
